# V5.5a Similar-Sector Initial Model Result

Date: 2026-07-18

## Result

Three V5.5 initial models were built and formally validated:

| Sector | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Telecom operators | Research signal exists, but A-share sample size is too small for strong IC-driven validation. |
| Gas / water operators | Workflow replicated, but the first composite does not improve on high dividend. Return to Research. |
| Port / rail infrastructure | Strongest initial evidence. Promote to next Research -> Quant loop with operating-state data. |

## Research Lesson

Stable cash-flow / dividend sectors can reuse the V5.1 and V5.4 workflow, but each sector still needs its own operating evidence:

| Sector | Missing specialist knowledge |
| --- | --- |
| Telecom | ARPU, users, capex cycle, EBITDA margin, enterprise-service mix. |
| Gas / water | Operating purity, tariff / spread, receivables, recurring utility revenue share. |
| Port / rail | Throughput, freight volume, trade state, tariff policy, regional exposure. |
