## Core Thesis

Insurance companies may support value investing when low valuation is backed by durable insurance franchise quality, solvency strength and rate-state-adjusted investment returns.

The key insurance question is:

```text
Is low valuation supported by durable insurance franchise quality, solvency strength and sustainable investment economics?
```

## Why Insurance Is Not A Bank Clone

Banks are mainly driven by credit quality, net interest margin, deposit franchise and capital adequacy.

Insurance companies are driven by:

- liability duration;
- embedded value and new business value;
- underwriting quality;
- claim and expense control;
- solvency adequacy;
- investment yield and asset-liability matching;
- interest-rate and equity-market sensitivity.

## Life Insurance Logic

Life insurers may benefit from:

- embedded value growth;
- new business value recovery;
- durable agency / bancassurance channels;
- higher long-term rates improving reinvestment economics;
- solvency strength.

They may fail when:

- new business value weakens;
- surrender or persistency deteriorates;
- long-term rates fall;
- equity-market losses pressure investment income;
- reported profit is driven by temporary investment gains.

## P&C Insurance Logic

P&C insurers may benefit from:

- low combined ratio;
- stable claim ratio;
- expense discipline;
- premium growth;
- disciplined underwriting.

They may fail when:

- claim ratio rises;
- catastrophe or one-off claim events occur;
- price competition weakens underwriting margin;
- investment income masks underwriting deterioration.

## Value Trap Risks

Low PB or low PE may indicate:

- undervalued insurance franchise;
- pessimism about rate cycle;
- underappreciated solvency strength.

It may also indicate:

- weak new business value;
- falling embedded value expectations;
- low investment yield;
- poor claim experience;
- solvency pressure;
- accounting profit not backed by operating quality.

## Research Position

Initial V5.3 hypotheses are plausible but unproven.

No insurance factor may be accepted until PIT validation shows financial explanation, statistical stability and robustness across rate and equity-market states.
