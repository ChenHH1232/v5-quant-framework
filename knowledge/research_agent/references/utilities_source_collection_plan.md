## Purpose

This file defines the first-pass source collection order for the V5.1 utilities test. It is a preparation document, not validation evidence and not a strategy decision.

## Collection Order

1. Official industry definitions and listed-company industry classification.
2. Company annual reports and exchange disclosures.
3. JoinQuant / DataJQ / Tushare field availability and point-in-time visibility.
4. Securities-company or industry research reports for business-model context.
5. Academic papers on utilities, dividend policy, regulated assets, leverage, and cash-flow quality.
6. Market commentary only as hypothesis material, not as evidence.

## Preferred Sources

- Exchange disclosure pages and annual reports for company facts.
- JoinQuant / DataJQ / Tushare for structured PIT-usable fields.
- Official statistical or regulatory sources for industry definitions.
- Research reports for business-model language and risk taxonomy.
- Academic papers for factor logic and validation methods.

## Sources Requiring Caution

- User-generated market commentary may help discover hypotheses but must not be treated as evidence.
- Websites with anti-crawling controls should be reviewed manually or through permitted APIs only.
- Scraped text must not be used when source terms prohibit automated collection.
- Broad high-dividend SOE articles should be tagged as style-basket material, not utilities-sector evidence.

## Minimum Output

Research Agent should produce:

- utilities universe definition;
- utilities data field map;
- utilities value-investing framework;
- utilities core factor hypothesis list;
- utilities validation handoff.

## Guardrails

- Do not tune on 2021-2026 platform results.
- Do not mix utilities with cross-industry high-dividend SOE baskets.
- Do not transfer bank-specific quality indicators without utilities-specific economic logic.
- Mark every claim as fact, hypothesis, source summary, or open question.
