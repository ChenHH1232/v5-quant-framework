# Highway V5.4g Reviewed Operating Result

Date: 2026-07-18

## Conclusion

V5.4g repaired the V5.4f data coverage blocker.

The strategy line is now strong enough to enter Engineering platform-replication preparation, but it is not an accepted strategy.

## Research Takeaways

The useful hypothesis is narrower than originally expected:

```text
High dividend is the main signal among toll-road operators. Reviewed operating disclosure and segment purity are best used as data-quality and business-purity gates, not as standalone alpha.
```

Evidence:

- dividend_yield mean IC: 0.2336;
- dividend_yield mean RankIC: 0.2875;
- high-dividend-only baseline: 76.86%;
- composite: 74.09%;
- rolling 2024: 30.84%;
- rolling 2025: 1.74%;
- rolling 2026: -2.67%.

## What Failed Or Stayed Weak

- Segment purity did not add clear incremental alpha after filtering the universe.
- Capex burden showed weak/negative IC.
- Reviewed operating disclosure score is useful for source reliability, not return prediction.
- Exact traffic volume, toll revenue and remaining concession values are still not promoted as numeric factors.

## Instruction To Research Agent

Do not tune weights to make the composite beat high-dividend-only.

Future research should focus on whether exact operating values can explain weak years:

- traffic volume growth;
- toll revenue growth;
- remaining concession maturity;
- toll policy changes;
- road-level vs company-level exposure.

Until exact values are reviewed, V5.4g should be described as:

```text
Highway high-dividend strategy with reviewed operating-data gate
```

not:

```text
Highway operating-alpha strategy
```
