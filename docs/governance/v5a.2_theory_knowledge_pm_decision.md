## Decision

V5a.2 is approved as a knowledge-base module for the enhanced dividend low-volatility and operating/free-cash-flow ETF roadmap.

It does not promote any strategy. It changes the default research routing:

- OCF / cash-flow quality + low volatility becomes the cross-sector core.
- Dividend sustainability remains required for the dividend sleeve.
- FCF becomes sector-gated enhancement, not a universal first factor.
- Low PB is demoted from global mainline to sector-specific valuation support.
- Momentum and mean reversion are support/state hypotheses only until PIT validation proves otherwise.

## Why

The theory and ETF/index review points in the same direction:

- Value works best when cheapness is tied to durable fundamentals.
- Momentum can diversify value, but must be turnover- and timing-audited.
- Mean reversion requires ex-ante proof that bad news is temporary.
- Low volatility is a real construction layer in dividend defensive products.
- Free cash flow is powerful but not equally comparable across all industries.
- Real ETFs and A-share indices use liquidity, dividend continuity, low volatility, positive cash flow, quality and sector exclusions rather than one raw high-yield factor.
