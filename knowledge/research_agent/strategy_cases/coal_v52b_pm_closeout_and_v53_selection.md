## New Governance Rule

Cyclical sectors must pass the cyclical data gate before formal modeling:

- commodity price state;
- production / inventory / supply-demand state;
- spread / margin state;
- company business-exposure PIT tags.

Without these layers, PM may allow data probes but must block formal strategy-candidate promotion.

## V5.3 Selection

Reason:

- insurance is close enough to banks to test financial-sector transfer;
- insurance has distinct variables, including embedded value, new business value, solvency, investment yield, rate state and underwriting quality;
- insurance avoids immediately repeating the unresolved commodity-cycle data problem.
