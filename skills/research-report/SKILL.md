# Research Report Skill

Use this skill to generate V5 research reports from saved experiment artifacts.

## Required Content

- Strategy objective and specification summary.
- Data version and code version.
- Leakage audit result.
- Factor research summary.
- Rolling validation result.
- Executable backtest assumptions.
- Candidate comparison.
- Acceptance or rejection reason.
- Known limitations and next checks.

## Tone

Reports should be clear enough to revisit months later and understand why the strategy was accepted, rejected, or left pending.
