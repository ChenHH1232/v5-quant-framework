## What Was Learned

The best coal branch was:

```text

```

Core factor interpretation:

- OCF yield is the cleanest cash-flow factor.
- FCF yield is useful but noisy because capex is cyclical and expansion-sensitive.
- Low PB and low PE remain supportive, not sufficient alone.
- Dividend is not the main coal alpha.
