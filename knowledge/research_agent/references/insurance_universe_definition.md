## Universe Principle

V5.3 uses an insurance operating-company universe.

It is not a broad financial-sector basket and not a high-dividend style basket.

## Include

- A-share listed life insurance companies;
- A-share listed property and casualty insurance companies;
- listed insurance groups where insurance is the dominant economic exposure;
- insurance-led holdings only when insurance is the core source of revenue, profit or embedded value.

## Exclude

- banks;
- brokers;
- trust companies;
- pure asset managers;
- insurance technology vendors;
- diversified financial holdings where insurance is not dominant;
- companies whose insurance exposure is historical, minor or not PIT-verifiable.

## Subgroups

Initial subgroup labels:

- `life_insurance`;
- `pnc_insurance`;
- `insurance_group`;
- `insurance_led_holding`;
- `manual_review_required`.

## PIT Rule

Universe membership must use information visible at or before the rebalance date.

Business-exposure evidence should come from:

- annual reports;
- interim reports;
- solvency reports;
- exchange announcements;
- official company disclosures.

Current business descriptions may only be used as a starting point and must not overwrite historical PIT tags.
